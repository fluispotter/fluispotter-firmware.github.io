Battery endurance test — run to power failure (2026-07-16 → 2026-07-18)

Owner test: 1100 mAh rechargeable battery, run until power fails, to characterize low-battery failure. fw 0.1.114 / ULP 1.12.0 / IMG-4. Evidence: ~/Downloads/fluispotter_battery_forensics_2026-07-18/ (hub timeline + full on-device capture, SHA-256 manifest; read-only protocol, REPL caught before the state machine on USB attach).

Headline numbers

  • Battery life: ~44 h 10 m (session prep ~2026-07-16 17:20 UTC → death between 13:29:21 and 13:37:29 on 2026-07-18).
  • Average draw: ≈ 24.9 mA (1100 mAh / 44.2 h) — matches the 20-25 mA estimate. Load profile: 22-sample session (38.4 h) + 5.7 h post-FINISH flush-cycling (2 min pump RUN + 8 min lightsleep REST, SWR-248).

How low-battery death actually looks (the test's question)

  1. No brownout-reset storm. Zero boot events in the entire final 28 h of the store, zero reboots in the whole session. The expected sag→reset→inrush→sag loop never happened.
  2. Silent stop during lightsleep. The final record (13:29:21, 'Disabling blood pump') is a perfectly orderly flush-cycle end: pump parked at the dead-point, disabled, then the 8-minute nap — and the board never woke. The next cycle (due ~13:37:29) never ran. No fatal-error append, no watchdog, no distress event.
  3. Safe final posture by construction: line sealed (BP parked at dead-point), all actuators off, asleep. For a patient scenario this is the best possible failure state.
  4. The motor worked to the end — a full clean 2-minute flush at 13:27-13:29, no stalls or warnings. RTC heartbeats sane to the last (13:29:04, agreeing with wall time).
  5. Zero data loss. Hub max uid == device last uid (16723458): a final sync succeeded after the last log line; the access point saw the station leave at 13:40:46.
  6. ~5-hour early-warning signature exists: 30 'Sync failed: TimeoutError()' events, ALL within the final ~5.2 h (first 08:19:49) — Wi-Fi transmit degrades first as voltage sags, while pumping and logging stay healthy. Syncs kept succeeding intermittently between timeouts (which is why nothing was lost).
  7. On the hub, death = the OFFLINE badge (13:40:46) with state frozen at FINISH — no alarm of any kind (nothing was designed to fire; see below).

Design questions raised (owner decisions)

  • Low-battery detection/warning (candidate SWR-287): the device has no battery-voltage telemetry; the only observable precursor is the sync-timeout burst. Options: (a) hardware battery measurement in the next revision; (b) firmware warning event on N consecutive sync timeouts ("possible low battery"); (c) hub-side alert on the same pattern. A deliberate low-battery shutdown (park + power off via the latch) would turn the accidental-but-clean death into a guaranteed one.
  • The post-FINISH flush period (5.7 h here) consumes battery at the full duty; unattended finished sessions on battery will always end in battery death unless collected or auto-shut-down.

Disposition

  • fw 0.1.115 flashed after evidence capture (SWR-277 auto-clean + SWR-278 in-session commands), FWVER verified.
  • Session data intact on the hub; session report (signed) already generated at FINISH.
  • Board resumes FINISH on power; conclude with reset_session before the next session (standing discipline).
