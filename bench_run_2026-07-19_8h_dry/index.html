The one blemish: WDT reset at ~03:37 UTC (05:37 local)

Sample 9's final dose completed 03:36:38 and its complete-event was committed by the post-dose sync; the WDT (cause 3) fired ~30–60 s later in the FLUSH-CYCLE-RESTART window after the sample — the window adjacent to the post-dose kick∥sync stack SWR-265 serialized (that window itself stayed clean for the 10th time tonight). Recovery was exact per design: state.dat already said sample 10, the device resumed SESSION 03:38:13, took samples 10–14 cleanly and FINISHed on schedule. Cost: the RAM-buffered 'Sample 9 complete' + 'Sample 10 scheduled in …' events were lost (audit-trail gap only — all 3 of sample 9's doses are evidenced with timestamps; the report's samples table shows all 15 loads). Rate: 1 fire in ~75 sample-ends across the recent proof runs.

Follow-up (tracked): extend the SWR-265 serialization to the post-sample flush-restart window and/or commit the event store before returning to IDLE so a reset there can never drop the sample-complete record.

Run concluded via reset_session 06:28:58 (store uid-continuous).
